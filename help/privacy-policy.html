At Vedha Varshini Wellness, we are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with us via WhatsApp or email.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or express interest in our services:

  • Your name, mobile number, and email address
  • Your health goals and dietary preferences (only if shared voluntarily)
  • Your delivery address (only when subscribing to a plan)
  • General browsing data (non-personally identifiable) through standard web analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support via WhatsApp or email
  • Personalize your wellness plan recommendations
  • Deliver subscription products to your address
  • Send relevant health tips, offers, and updates (only if you opt-in)

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your details are used solely to deliver our wellness services to you. We may share minimal information with delivery partners solely for the purpose of completing your order.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of any marketing communications at any time

6. Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies for basic functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies if you prefer.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites such as Instagram and YouTube. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their respective privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
